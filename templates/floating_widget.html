How It Works

The floating widget appears in the bottom-right corner of your page. You can click on it to open the chat, or drag it around the screen. When you're done, simply minimize it back to a bubble. All your messages are preserved, and the widget respects your preferences.

Getting Started

To embed this widget on your website, simply add this single line of code:

The widget is fully customizable. You can change colors, position, size, welcome message, and more. All configuration is done through JavaScript, making it incredibly flexible for your needs.
